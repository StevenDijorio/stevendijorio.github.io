Selected engineering projects

Analog spiking-neuron hardware

I designed and assembled the circuit board, bench-tested it with an oscilloscope and function generator, and compared the measured waveforms with NGSpice and COMSOL simulations. I analyzed spike trains in Python and MATLAB.

Wearable stretch-sensor prototype

I built the team’s Wheatstone-bridge sensor circuit and used Arduino Uno serial voltage readings to troubleshoot bridge imbalance.

Adaptive toothbrush holder

I documented requirements, compared three concepts with a weighted decision matrix, and used fit checks to inform a crown-strap revision. I applied standards knowledge while reviewing design choices.
