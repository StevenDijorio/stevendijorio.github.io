Biliary injury in liver transplantation

Cytomegalovirus and biliary complications

Observational studies have reported differing associations between cytomegalovirus infection and biliary complications after transplantation. One retrospective study did not find an independent association with non-anastomotic strictures. Differences in definitions and cohorts limit comparison.
